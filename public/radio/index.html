Advanced Controls
Simple shows only a Stop button. Advanced adds skip back/forward and a sleep timer that stops playback after a set time.
Haptic Feedback
Vibrates briefly on button taps. Only available on mobile devices that support it.
Voice Announcements
Speaks aloud what is happening — "Now playing…", "Paused", "Stopped", or that a station is not available. To change the voice or improve quality, use OS Settings below (Accessibility → Text-to-Speech).
Audio Tones
Plays a short rising tone when a stream starts or resumes, and a falling tone when paused.
Auto-Resume
When the app starts, automatically begin playing the station that was playing when the app closed. If the radio was paused or stopped, or that station has since been removed, the app starts stopped.
Audio Streams
Stations are run by other people, and this app has no control over what they play. Listen to a station yourself before adding it for someone else.
Device
Live Caption transcribes audio playing on the device in real time — enable it in Accessibility settings. OS Settings gives access to everything else: Wi-Fi, text-to-speech voice, display brightness, and more.
To change Wi-Fi, captions or the speaking voice, open this device's Settings app.
