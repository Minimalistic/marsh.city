The home-screen and install icons in `public/icons/` (and the browser tab's icon) are whole-number nearest-neighbor rescales of our own player drawing (`public/player.png`, from `art/sprites.json`), generated by `tools/make-icons.js`.

Original to DailyDungeon (no third-party license): the spinning coin on the win screen, `public/img/coin-0.svg` to `coin-3.svg`, drawn from the `content.json` palette. The title screen's background, `public/bg.png`, is a floor drawn from our own sprites by `tools/sprite-lab.js` (it replaced the boilerplate's Kenney-style picture on 2026-10-18).
