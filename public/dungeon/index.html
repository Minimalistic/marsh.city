      How to play

      Get down

      Every floor has a ladder. Find it and climb down. The locals have other plans.

      Move

      Tap a tile to walk there. Tap yourself to wait a turn. Swipe from yourself to take one step, diagonals too, but never around a wall's corner.

      Fight

      Tap a monster beside you to hit it. Let them come to you: whoever stands their ground swings better.

      Look

      Touch and hold anything to look at it: what it is, how hard it hits, what it's up to. Rude to stare, but it beats guessing.

      Loot

      Walk into a chest to open it. Walk into urns, springs and shrines to see what they do. Some charge for it.

      Use things

      Pack opens what you carry. Tap anything there to use it, wear it or drop it. Log shows what just happened, in case it happened fast.

      Heal

      HP doesn't come back on its own. Health potions, bandages and food mend you, and so does the Ember Ring. Ration accordingly.

      Traps

      Stand beside a trap you've spotted and a Disarm button shows your odds. It takes a turn. Miss, and it goes off. On you.

      Omens

      Most floors have one, named next to your HP. Tap it to find out what kind of day it is.

      Dying

      Die in practice and Continue? starts the floor over, with a mark on you. What the floor gave you waits in a grave sack where you fell. Die again before you reach it and it's gone.

      Today's floor is one try. Die there and the floor keeps what it gave you, most of its XP too.

      Playing with a keyboard?

      Arrow keys, W A S D or the numpad step. Q E Z C or Home, PgUp, End and PgDn go diagonal. Space, the period key or numpad 5 waits a turn. Want buttons on screen? Settings > Controls > D-pad.
