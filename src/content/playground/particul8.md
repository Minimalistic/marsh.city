title: Particul8
description: Type anything - words, emoji, both - and watch a cloud of particles morph from one shape to the next. Every slider previews live.
---

Type in the box. Each word (or character, or sentence, your call) becomes a shape, and a few thousand particles spring from one shape to the next. Emoji bring their own colors. Everything below the stage previews live, so drag things around until it looks right, then copy a link that reproduces it exactly.

Video and GIF export are coming. For now it renders in the browser and the share link is the file.
