I'm Jason - Director of Technology at Lighthouse Center for Vital Living, an assistive technology & older adult services nonprofit in
Duluth, Minnesota, with 13+ years in IT. I manage infrastructure, lead assistive
technology services, and run an AT lending library. The people we serve are older
adults, people with disabilities, and anyone who needs help navigating technology.
I hold an Assistive Technology Applications Certificate from CSUN.

Outside that job I build software on my own time. The biggest is
[WhatCanHelp](/projects/whatcanhelp/), a free tool for finding assistive
technology. Smaller ones: a [radio app](/projects/radiogridxl/) piloted with
older adults at my organization, a [Raspberry Pi pet feeder](/projects/petfeedr/),
and a [voice-memo-to-notes pipeline](/projects/echoetcher/). Most of it is Node,
SQLite and plain JavaScript, with Python for machine learning and automation.

I've been fascinated with computers since I was a kid - learned to fix them
because I broke them. Art and design started in my teens, which led to a
B.S. in Game Art & Design, then time as a concept artist doing noir-style
western illustrations. Software came through years of self-teaching after
that. The whole path still shapes how I work: I think visually, I design
for the person using the thing, and I see projects through from architecture
to interface.

For a more detailed look at my experience and skills, check out my [resume](/resume/).

## How I build

I orchestrate, AI implements. Most of what's here - the production apps, this site, the
project pages - I build by working through [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) in
the terminal. I make the calls on architecture, design, and tradeoffs; I read what comes
back, test it, and send it back when it's wrong. It's still programming, and I'm
responsible for what ships. [More on how the site is built.](/oak/)
